The activity feed on your Sifa ID profile is opinionated. We do not show everything you do on [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com). We show the things you made, not the things you reacted to.

The rules come from a taxonomy in the Sifa SDK. The tables below are generated from that taxonomy when this page is built, so they cannot drift out of sync with what the product actually does.

## How your activity gets here

You do not connect anything. Your activity follows your [DID](/docs/dids-usernames-providers), so when you use an app on the Atmosphere, the records it writes to your provider are yours already. Sifa reads them and renders the ones that count as work.

Start using a new app and it shows up on your profile on its own. Stop using one and it stops appearing. There is no list to maintain and nothing to reconnect. The apps Sifa recognises are listed under [Supported apps](/docs/supported-apps).

Two things work differently:

- **Accounts off the Atmosphere** (GitHub, a Fediverse server, an RSS feed, ORCID) have no DID to follow, so you add and verify them once under [External accounts](/docs/external-accounts). After that they behave the same way.
- **Your own posts only.** Sifa reads what you published, never what you saw. See below.

## What we leave out: "Did"

Things you did on the network: likes, follows, reposts, RSVPs. We keep these off your public profile so the page stays a record of work, not a record of taps. The [Bluesky](https://bsky.app) app and the originating apps already show this side of you.

A third tier (`filtered`) covers configuration records, moderation records, and other infrastructure that we hide from every Sifa surface. Those records still live on your PDS, we just do not render them.

## Why it is set up this way

Most professional networks rank, score, or pad your profile with engagement metrics. We do not. Your profile is a record of work you did. If you want to know if someone is good at what they say they do, click through to the actual work.

## Want a different filter?

You cannot currently customise which categories show up on your profile. If there is a record type you think we should add or remove, open an issue against [sifa-sdk](https://github.com/singi-labs/sifa-sdk) with a link to the lexicon and a short reason.
