Each weekday, Sifa ID shows one member profile in the featured slot on the [sifa.id](https://sifa.id) homepage. The [@sifa.id](https://bsky.app/profile/sifa.id) Bluesky account also posts about that profile.

Featuring is **opt-in**. Sifa never features your profile without your permission. The setting is off when you create an account.

## Turn featuring on or off

1. Log in to Sifa.
2. Go to [Settings -> Privacy](https://sifa.id/settings/privacy).
3. Find the **Featured profile** section.
4. Set the **Feature my profile** toggle to on or off.

The change is immediate. You can change the setting at any time.

When the toggle is on, the section also shows your status:

- **Scheduled to be featured**: Sifa picked your profile for a day in the next week. {/* ste-allow: S4 quoting the status label in Sifa settings */}
- **Not yet scheduled**: Sifa did not pick your profile yet.

If your profile qualifies and featuring is off, Sifa can show a banner on your own profile page. Click **Include me** on the banner to turn featuring on. Click **Not now** to close the banner.

## Who qualifies

Sifa selects only from profiles that meet all of these conditions:

- Featuring is on in your settings.
- Your account is active. Sifa never features a deactivated account.
- Your profile has a profile photo.
- Your profile has at least 7 of the 8 items in the list below.

The 8 items are:

1. A profile photo
2. A headline
3. An about text
4. A current position
5. A past position
6. Three or more skills
7. An education entry
8. An [external account](/docs/external-accounts)

If your profile does not qualify, Sifa does not select it, even when the toggle is on. Add the missing items. Sifa can then select your profile.

## How Sifa selects a profile

Sifa selects profiles at random from all profiles that qualify. Sifa features profiles on weekdays only (Monday to Friday, UTC).

You can get featured more than one time. Between two features of the same profile, there are at least 6 months.

## Notifications

Sifa sends two notifications in the app:

- **Your profile qualifies to be featured**: your profile meets the conditions for the first time. {/* ste-allow: S4 quoting the notification title in Sifa */}
- **You are up next to be featured**: Sifa scheduled your profile for a day. This notification comes about 7 days before that day. {/* ste-allow: S4 quoting the notification title in Sifa */}

You can change these notifications in [Settings -> Notifications](https://sifa.id/settings/notifications).

## Stop a scheduled feature

Sifa scheduled your profile and you do not want the feature? Turn off the **Feature my profile** toggle. Sifa then removes your profile from the schedule.

A featured day starts at 00:00 UTC. At that time, the homepage shows the featured profile. At 12:00 UTC, the @sifa.id account posts about the profile on Bluesky.

- Turn off the toggle before 00:00 UTC on your day. Sifa does not feature your profile.
- Turn off the toggle on your day, before 12:00 UTC. Sifa does not post about your profile on Bluesky. The homepage shows your profile until the end of that day.

Turn on the toggle again when you want Sifa to select your profile again.

## What a feature shows

A feature shows only data from your public profile. See [Privacy and data](/docs/privacy-and-data) for what is public on Sifa.
