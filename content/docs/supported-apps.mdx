Sifa reads your activity from the apps below. You do not connect any of them: your records live with your [DID](/docs/dids-usernames-providers), so using an app is all it takes. Start using one and it appears on your profile on its own.

## Why your app might not be here

The Atmosphere is growing faster than any one list. If an app you use is missing, its records are still safely on your provider, Sifa just does not recognise them yet and will not render them.

Adding one is usually a small change. Open an issue on [sifa-workspace](https://github.com/singi-labs/sifa-workspace/issues) with the app's name and its lexicon namespace.

## Why some of your activity is missing, even from a listed app

Being on this list means Sifa reads the app, not that everything you do there reaches your profile. Two filters apply.

**We show what you made, not what you did.** Posts, articles, photos, repositories, reviews and merged pull requests are work. Likes, follows, reposts and RSVPs are taps. The second kind stays off your profile so the page reads as a record of work. The full split is on the [activity feed](/docs/activity-feed) page.

**Configuration records never appear.** Preferences, moderation lists, block lists and similar plumbing are hidden everywhere on Sifa. They are still yours and still on your provider; they just are not activity.

One consequence worth knowing: an app can be on this list and still show nothing on your profile, if everything you have done there falls into one of those two buckets.

## Sources that are not on the Atmosphere

These have no records on your provider, so unlike the apps above, you add and verify them once. After that they behave the same way.

| Source                       | What Sifa reads                                                | Setup                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| GitHub                       | Merged pull requests you authored, in public repositories      | [Connect a GitHub account](/docs/connect-github)       |
| Fediverse                    | Your public posts from Mastodon, GoToSocial, Pleroma, Pixelfed | [Connect a Fediverse account](/docs/connect-fediverse) |
| ORCID                        | Your academic publications                                     | [External accounts](/docs/external-accounts)           |
| RSS, Substack, personal site | Items from the feed                                            | [External accounts](/docs/external-accounts)           |

Anything else you link, LinkedIn or YouTube or an arbitrary URL, is shown as a link on your profile and never polled.
